## B16. Survey the current state-of-the-art solutions in cybersecurity.

### a. Agentic AI Systems
- Agentic AI systems can autonomously make decisions, plan tasks, and perform actions with minimal human input  
- Unlike traditional chatbots, they can interact with tools, APIs, databases, and external applications to complete multi-step tasks  
- Agentic AI can improve efficiency by automating workflows, research, scheduling, and decision-making tasks  
- These systems can also introduce risks such as prompt injection, unauthorised actions, data exposure, and tool misuse if not properly secured  
- (E.g. AI agents autonomously managing schedules or interacting with enterprise systems)  

Reference:  
https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/agentic-ai#2095054954

### b. Automated Security Operations Centers (SOC)
- Automated SOC systems use AI and automation to help monitor and respond to cybersecurity threats in real time  
- These systems can automatically analyse logs, detect suspicious behaviour, and prioritise security alerts  
- SOC automation helps organisations respond to phishing, malware, ransomware, and unusual network activity faster  
- Automated workflows reduce manual workload and allow analysts to focus on more serious threats  
- (E.g. Automatically detecting suspicious login attempts and triggering incident response actions)
