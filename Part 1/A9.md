## A9. Discover privacy technique used online

- **Cookies (Tracking):**
  - Online privacy techniques are used to control how personal data is collected, stored, and shared
  - Websites use cookies to store information about users, such as preferences or login sessions
  - Different types of cookies exist, including necessary cookies (for functionality) and tracking cookies (for analytics and advertising)
  - Users can manage their privacy by controlling cookie settings, using private browsing, or limiting data sharing
  - These techniques help protect personal information and give users control over how their data is used
  - (E.g. Websites asking for cookie consent and allowing users to accept or reject tracking cookies)
