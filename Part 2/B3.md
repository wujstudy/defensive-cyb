## B3. Discover 3 proactive security implementations in practice.

### a. Network Segmentation
- Network segmentation divides networks into separate isolated sections  
- This limits how far attackers can move if one system is compromised  
- Helps protect sensitive systems and reduce the overall attack surface  
- Commonly used to separate guest Wi-Fi, staff systems, and business networks  
- (E.g. Separating guest Wi-Fi from internal POS systems)  

Reference:  
https://zeronetworks.com/blog/network-segmentation-all-you-need-to-know

### b. Proactive Security Monitoring
- Proactive security focuses on detecting and preventing threats before attacks happen  
- Continuous monitoring and risk assessments help identify vulnerabilities early  
- Helps organisations respond to suspicious activity before systems are compromised  
- Improves overall security posture and reduces risk of successful attacks  
- (E.g. Regular vulnerability scanning and threat hunting in enterprise environments)  

Reference:  
https://cymulate.com/cybersecurity-glossary/proactive-security/

### c. Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
- SIEM systems collect and analyse logs from multiple systems in real time  
- Suspicious activity and unusual behaviour can be detected early  
- Centralised monitoring improves visibility across enterprise environments  
- Helps security teams investigate and respond to threats faster  
- (E.g. Splunk or Microsoft Sentinel detecting abnormal login attempts)  

Reference:  
https://www.sentinelone.com/cybersecurity-101/data-and-ai/what-is-security-information-and-event-management-siem/
