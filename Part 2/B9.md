## B9. Participate in an industry-related cybersecurity event.

- Discussed how cybercriminals are using automation and AI to launch attacks faster and on a larger scale  
- Ransomware was highlighted as a major ongoing threat causing financial and operational disruption to organisations  
- Covered how phishing and credential theft attacks are becoming more advanced and difficult to identify  
- Threat intelligence, continuous monitoring, and rapid incident response were discussed as important proactive security practices  
- Emphasised the importance of cybersecurity awareness, regular patching, and stronger defensive security strategies
