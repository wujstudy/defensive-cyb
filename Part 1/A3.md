## A3. Discover security concepts used in your house

- **Concept:** CIA + Authentication + Authorisation + Enforcement Mechanism

- **Confidentiality:**
  - Limits access to private spaces and networks
  - (E.g. Locked doors and password-protected Wi-Fi, password name is purposely using letters from the end of the alphabet to appear lower on the list of networks)

- **Integrity:**
  - Password protection prevents unauthorised changes
  - (E.g. Home Wi-Fi settings protected by admin password)

- **Availability:**
  - Ensures home systems and devices are accessible when needed
  - Maintains functionality of essential services
  - (E.g. Internet connection and electricity available for daily use)
