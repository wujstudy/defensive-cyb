•	A1. Discover security concepts used on campus 
o	Concept: CIA + Authentication + Authorisation + Enforcement Mechanism
  	Confidentiality: 
  	Restricts access to sensitive areas and information 
  	Ensures only authorised users can view/access data 
  	(E.g. Student ID card required to enter buildings or labs) 
	Integrity: 
  	Prevents unauthorised modification of data or systems 
  	Monitoring systems help detect tampering or suspicious activity 
  	(E.g. CCTV cameras monitoring campus activity to detect incidents)
	Availability: 
  	Ensures systems and services are accessible when needed 
  	Supports continuous access to resources 
  	(E.g. Campus Wi-Fi and emergency phones available for students)
