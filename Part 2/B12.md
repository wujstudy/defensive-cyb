## B12. Discover two bias cases when using a generative AI system.

### a. Gender Bias in AI Image Generation
- Generative AI systems can show bias when generating images for certain professions or roles  
- AI models often associate jobs like CEOs or engineers with men, while associating nurses or assistants with women  
- This happens because AI models are trained on datasets that may reflect real-world stereotypes  
- Biased outputs can reinforce unfair assumptions and reduce representation in AI-generated content  
- (E.g. AI image generators mainly producing male images for prompts like “CEO” or “software engineer”)  

Reference:  
https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2023-generative-ai-bias/

### b. Racial and Cultural Bias in Generative AI
- Generative AI systems can produce biased or stereotypical outputs relating to race, ethnicity, or culture  
- Some demographic groups may be overrepresented or underrepresented depending on training data biases  
- Bias in AI systems can negatively affect fairness, inclusivity, and representation  
- Training data biases can lead to discriminatory or inaccurate AI-generated outputs  
- (E.g. AI systems generating stereotypical images or descriptions for certain ethnic groups)
