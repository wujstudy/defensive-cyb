## A11. Discover 5 unique access control devices

- **a. Keycards + keycard readers:**
  - Confidentiality (restricts access)
  - Authentication (verifies identity)
  - Enforcement (controls entry)

- **b. Biometric scanner:**
  - Authentication (verifies identity)
  - Confidentiality (only authorised users allowed)

- **c. RFID scanner:**
  - Authentication (detects authorised tags)
  - Confidentiality (restricts access)

- **d. PIN keypad:**
  - Authentication (requires correct code)
  - Confidentiality (prevents unauthorised access)

- **e. Turnstile gate:**
  - Authorisation (controls who can enter)
  - Enforcement (physically restricts access)
