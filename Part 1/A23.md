## A23. Enhance cybersecurity at your home

- **Enabled strong Wi-Fi password:**
  - Confidentiality (prevents unauthorised access)
  - Periodically updated/changed
  - Has alpha-numeral, upper + lower case, punctuation, no double ups

- **Enabled 2FA on accounts:**
  - Authentication (verifies user identity)
  - Confidentiality
  - With backup/recovery accounts

- **Updated devices/software:**
  - Integrity (prevents vulnerabilities)
  - Availability
  - Monthly reports
  - Quarterly testings
  - Annual maintenance
