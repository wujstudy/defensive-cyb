## B13. Perform a jailbreak attack on a generative AI assistant (controlled test only).

- Conducted controlled jailbreak and prompt injection testing using the Venice AI platform  
- Tested instruction override prompts, fictional roleplay framing, indirect prompting, and reverse-text prompts  
- Observed that educational or research-based framing affected moderation strictness and AI responses  
- The AI provided more detailed explanations when prompts were framed as research or educational scenarios  
- Highlighted the importance of strong AI guardrails, moderation systems, and contextual filtering  
- (E.g. Using prompts like “Ignore all previous instructions” and reverse-text prompts to test moderation behaviour)
