## B28. Produce a cyber safety flyer for elders, high school students, CEOs, or Uni students.

- Target audience: elderly people vulnerable to phishing scams and suspicious phone calls  
- Used large text and simple fonts to make the flyer easier to read  
- Used high contrast colours to improve visibility and readability  
- Kept information short, clear, and easy to understand using dot points  
- Included common scam warning signs such as urgent threats and requests for passwords or verification codes
